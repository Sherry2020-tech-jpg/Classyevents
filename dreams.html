✦ Dream Protocol

  • Keep your journal beside the bed — recall fades 90% within 10 minutes of waking.
  • Write in present tense ("I am flying") — it re-activates dream memory more strongly.
  • Do reality checks on triggers: doorways, phone checks, and every time something feels strange.
  • Set a gentle intention before sleep: "Tonight I will notice I am dreaming."
  • Wake back to bed after 5–6 hours of sleep — REM is longest then, prime time for lucidity.
  • Review your symbols weekly in the Compass — recurring themes are your mind's headlines.
